Fairy Forest Quest

One continuous forest adventure with five different English obstacles. The answers are part of the world: they build bridges, light portals, charge crystals and open doors.

  • A/D or ←/→ — move
  • W / ↑ / Space — jump
  • Wrong answers cost a heart only when you deliberately land on or hit them.
  • The enchanted score and forest ambience can be adjusted from the Grove settings.
Controls & mechanics

How to Play

Each obstacle has one English blank and three choices. The selection method changes with the world:

  • Bridge stones — land from above.
  • Lantern gate — jump and hit a lantern from below.
  • Crystals — land on the crystal pedestal.
  • Rune door — jump and land on a floor rune.
  • Moon Gate — jump into the chosen magic orb.
